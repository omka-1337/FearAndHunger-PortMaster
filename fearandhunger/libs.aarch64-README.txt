These are the libraries NW.js needs and handheld firmware does not ship.

Chromium links against a desktop system: X11, NSS, ATK, AT-SPI, CUPS, GBM and
xkbcommon, plus what those pull in. On KNULLI (RG40XX-V, gladiator-ii) every one
of the sixteen it asks for was absent, so the port carries them and their whole
dependency closure, 57 libraries in all.

They are stock Debian bookworm arm64 binaries, unmodified, listed with their
source packages in MANIFEST.txt. Rebuild the folder with ./fetch-libs.sh.

Libraries the firmware does have are deliberately not overridden: glib, gobject,
gio, cairo, pango, dbus, expat, udev and alsa stay the system's own.

libgbm, libdrm and libwayland-server are here only for firmware without them.
libgbm loads the firmware's Mesa driver and has to come from the same Mesa build,
so wherever the firmware has its own the launcher preloads those instead, and
the copies here are never loaded. Debian's libgbm from Mesa 22.3 crashed inside
gbm_create_device against ROCKNIX's current Mesa.

If the launcher still reports something missing on your device, send the list.
